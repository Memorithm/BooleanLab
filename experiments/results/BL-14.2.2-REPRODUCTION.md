## What the experiment does not isolate

Predicate zero includes the magnitude-top-four mask. Consequently the Boolean family contains that numerical heuristic, so minimum Boolean SEARCH loss cannot exceed magnitude SEARCH loss by construction. This says nothing about generalization: VALIDATION can still worsen, as unequal_scale/seed 0 demonstrates.

The Boolean path also selects across multiple topologies using SEARCH labels, whereas the numerical heuristics do not optimize masks against those labels. A favourable validation cell therefore cannot isolate an advantage of Boolean algebra from an advantage of discrete mask search. A separate experiment needs matched search budgets and stronger numerical/discrete-search controls. Do not retune this executed design to obtain a positive outcome.

As a descriptive diagnostic only, compare the selected Boolean mask to the smallest validation MSE among magnitude, unit-level 2:4 and activation-energy in each trial. Full-precision values give better/equal/worse counts `1/6/5`. That post-hoc envelope is NOT a new deployable baseline or a validation-selected policy. The canonical result's direct pairwise counts, all twelve cells, random controls and dense comparison remain authoritative.

The three regimes at a given seed share underlying draws; twelve cells are not twelve independent statistical populations. No confidence interval, significance claim, speedup or universal sparsity benefit is inferred from these counts. Representative learned hidden features, other retained densities, matched search effort, per-instance uncertainty and full controller/hardware accounting remain separate future work.
