## Interpretation boundary

This experiment establishes a reproducible bounded Boolean-only comparison population. It does **not** approximate the full eight-input Boolean-function space and it does **not** prove that functions absent from the population require hybrid mathematics.

The current sedenion control includes functions with nonlinearity 98, 99 and 100, and the validated `GF(2^8)` inversion components have nonlinearity 112. Those values exceed the best nonlinearity 96 observed in this bounded baseline, but this is not evidence of novelty or superiority because construction costs and stronger equivalence relations have not yet been matched. BL-13.2.1 must perform that screening explicitly.
