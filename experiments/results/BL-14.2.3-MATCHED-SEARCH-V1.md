## Comparison contract

The [pre-execution audit design](../BL-14.2.3-MATCHED-SEARCH-PROTOCOL.md) keeps the three existing regimes and four seeds, adding retained counts 2/8, 4/8 and 6/8, for 36 paired exploratory cells. The fixed nonlinear feature layer is not trained; only its readout is trained. Each trial uses 128 TRAIN, 64 SEARCH and 64 VALIDATION-labelled development examples. All 36 cells are frozen before their validation batches are constructed.

BOOLEAN enumerates three-predicate functions, groups identical masks and spends one full SEARCH evaluation per unique eligible mask. If that gives K unique masks, DIRECT-MATCHED receives exactly K unique direct masks and the same SEARCH labels/scorer. Both contain the magnitude anchor. The rest of the direct population follows the single declared label-independent ordering; it is not chosen from several orders after observing losses.

DIRECT-SAME reconstructs exactly the Boolean-induced population using retained-index codes. DIRECT-ALL evaluates all 28/70/28 masks at the respective retained counts and is explicitly a larger-budget diagnostic. Dense and non-search magnitude, unit N:M and activation-energy heuristics remain separate controls. The full report includes every candidate, all generating truth-table codes, the exact scoring budgets, selected masks and losses.

Equal budget here means **unique-topology task scoring only**. It does not mean equal population construction, rule enumeration, training, statistics, controller evaluation, total arithmetic, memory traffic, energy or elapsed time. All exact minimum-SEARCH-MSE ties are retained by the implementation; the executed panel happened to have a single selected topology per search arm/cell.

## Matched-budget outcome

Lower validation-labelled development task MSE is better. Comparing BOOLEAN with DIRECT-MATCHED gives:

| Retained units | Boolean better | Equal | Boolean worse |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2/8 | 1 | 7 | 4 |
| 4/8 | 3 | 6 | 3 |
| 6/8 | 1 | 11 | 0 |
| All 36 cells | 5 | 24 | 7 |

These are descriptive counts for this particular, previously observed panel. Regimes at a given seed share base draws, and the three densities also share each fitted model. Neither statistical independence nor a population-level superiority claim follows from the table. A single fixed direct-population ordering does not exhaust competent direct-search methods.

**No general Boolean advantage is demonstrated at matched scoring budget.** Five favourable cells remain in the record, as do the seven unfavourable cells and 24 ties. Settings are not changed to improve this outcome.

## Representation-equivalence control

BOOLEAN and DIRECT-SAME return exactly the same selected mask identities and bit-identical SEARCH/VALIDATION metrics in all 36 cells. Both operate on the same exact topology set with the same scorer. This is the required null control: changing the encoding of the same masks does not create a quality advantage.

The distinction is between (a) syntax or truth-table identity, (b) the topology population induced by the predicates, and (c) the measured numerical behaviour. The current audit examines topology selection, not a trained Boolean controller's transfer to unseen models or input-conditioned routing.

## Dense comparison and larger-budget diagnostic

BOOLEAN has higher task MSE than dense in 35/36 cells. The sole slightly lower value occurs for `correlated`, seed 1, retained 6/8: dense `4.01831067341568010e-2`, sparse `3.95695489654522697e-2`. In that cell BOOLEAN, DIRECT-SAME, DIRECT-MATCHED, DIRECT-ALL, magnitude, unit N:M and activation energy all select the identical mask `125` (`0x7d`). This isolated improvement is therefore not specific to Boolean logic and does not establish general quality preservation.

Against the larger-budget DIRECT-ALL SEARCH winner, BOOLEAN is better/equal/worse on the validation-labelled development partition in 2/13/21 cells. DIRECT-ALL is checked to have no worse optimum on SEARCH than any subset. A lower SEARCH optimum does not guarantee a lower validation loss; the diagnostic never re-ranks candidates on validation.
