## Declared screen

Each of the 16 scalar coordinate functions produced by the pinned BL-13.2.0 sedenion control is compared against the **full exact-deduplicated BL-13.1.2 population**, not only its Pareto front.

The direct expanded screen recognizes only:

1. exact whole-truth-table equality;
2. whole-output complement;
3. exhaustive renaming/permutation of the eight input variables;
4. exhaustive input permutation followed by whole-output complement.

After those direct relations fail, the affine-invariant screen tests exact necessary invariants for the declared Boolean affine relation

`g(x) = f(Ax + b) XOR c`, with invertible `A`.

The necessary signature contains:

- input width;
- algebraic degree;
- output-complement-canonical Hamming weight;
- sorted multiset of absolute Walsh-spectrum coefficients.

A differing signature excludes the declared affine equivalence. A matching signature would remain **inconclusive** rather than establish equivalence. This screen does not test EA or CCZ equivalence.

## Interpretation boundary

This is a **negative equivalence-screen result**, not a novelty result. Within the frozen 1,685-function Boolean-only corpus, none of the 16 tested sedenion truth tables matches directly under the four declared direct relations, and the exact necessary invariants rule out the declared affine relation against every corpus member.

It does **not** show that these functions are new mathematical Boolean functions, new EA/CCZ equivalence classes, or superior constructions. The Boolean-only search is bounded, EA/CCZ and broader prior-art equivalence remain pending, construction costs are not yet matched, and prior-art review has not been completed.
